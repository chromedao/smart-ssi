# Trademarks

The code is open source; the names are not.

"Smart-SSI", "Chrome DAO", "CHROMES DAO" and their logos identify the attestations, apps and services run by
Chrome DAO. You may use the code under its licenses, and you may say that your project is based on or
compatible with Smart-SSI. You may not:

- publish an app, a service or attestations under these names or logos, or under a name that could be
  confused with them;
- suggest that Chrome DAO issues, endorses or audits your fork or your attestations.

A fork must use its own name, its own credential on the Solana Attestation Service and its own notary keys.
Verifiers decide which credentials they trust: attestations issued by a fork are not Smart-SSI attestations.
